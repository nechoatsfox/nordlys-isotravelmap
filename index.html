About the data

Travel times are modelled from published 2025–26 timetables (Vy, Go-Ahead Nordic, Flytoget, Skyss, Kolumbus, AKT, Kystbussen, NOR-WAY, Lavprisekspressen, SAS, Norwegian, Widerøe — the operators behind Entur's national dataset) and real road routes. Flights include realistic airport access, check-in and exit time. Transit waits assume a planned departure plus transfer buffers. Coastline: Natural Earth 1:10m. See README for sources.
